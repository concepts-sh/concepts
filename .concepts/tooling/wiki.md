---
title: Wiki
description: A disposable HTML view of a base, built from its files into the temp directory, never a store.
---
The wiki has a page per [concept](../format/concept.md) with its [connections](../format/connection.md) as sentences, backlinks, a reading order from prerequisites, a focus graph, and a health view. External concepts appear at the edge as ghost nodes. Edits go through the [skill](skill.md) or an editor, not the page; the wiki is rebuilt from the files every time, so it cannot disagree with them.

Example: `npx concepts wiki`.
Source: `README.md`, section Use.

## Connections
- Uses the files of a [base](../format/base.md) and nothing else.
- Not the [base](../format/base.md): the wiki is a view and never a store.
