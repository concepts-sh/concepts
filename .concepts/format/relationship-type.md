---
title: Relationship type
description: One of eight openers that names the kind of claim a connection makes.
---
The eight types in five families: Kind of and Part of (hierarchy), Same as (equivalence), Replaces (lifecycle), Not (contrast), Requires, Causes and Used for (association). Each has an opener from either side, such as Part of and Parts. The opener is the first word of the [connection](connection.md); the rest of the sentence is the claim. When a relationship needs its own definition, it is a [concept](concept.md).

Example: `Part of`, the opener of the first connection below.
Source: `SPEC.md`, section 3.

## Connections
- Used for a [connection](connection.md): the opener is its first word.
- Requires the [style](style.md) rule that one word has one meaning, applied to the openers.
