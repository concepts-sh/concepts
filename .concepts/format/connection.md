---
title: Connection
description: A sentence in a concept that states a claim about it and another concept, starting with a type opener and containing a link.
---
A connection is a claim a reader needs to use the [concept](concept.md) correctly. Any other mention of another concept is a [link](link.md) in the body, not a connection. Connections sit in a list under `## Connections` at the end of the file. A concept may have none; a link from another concept counts as much as one written here. A long list is a hint that the concept is two concepts.

Example: every line under `## Connections` in this file.
Source: `SPEC.md`, section 2.

## Connections
- Part of a [concept](concept.md), at the end of the file.
- Requires a [relationship type](relationship-type.md) as its first word.
- Not a [link](link.md): every connection contains a link, but a link outside the list is not a connection.
