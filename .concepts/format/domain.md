---
title: Domain
description: A folder inside a base that is the one home of each concept in it.
---
A domain answers "where would a person look for this". It does not state meaning; [connections](connection.md) do. A domain with more than about thirty [concepts](concept.md) gets its own [map](map.md).

Example: `.concepts/format/` and `.concepts/tooling/`.
Source: `SPEC.md`, section 1.

## Connections
- Part of a [base](base.md).
- Parts: [concepts](concept.md), each in exactly one domain.
