---
title: Style
description: The seven writing rules for titles, descriptions and connections, borrowed from the idea of Simplified Technical English.
---
One word has one meaning. A sentence stays under 25 words. Active voice, present tense. One idea per sentence. Must and must not for rules. No ambiguous "it" or "this". Exact names. The rules apply strictly to the title, the [description](description.md) and the [connections](connection.md); the body may hold examples, code and longer explanation. The dictionary of ASD-STE100 is not borrowed, only its idea.

Example: the description of this file, 18 words, one sentence.
Source: `SPEC.md`, section 4.

## Connections
- Used for the [description](description.md) and the [connections](connection.md), strictly, and for the body, loosely.
- Required by [relationship types](relationship-type.md): each opener has one meaning.
