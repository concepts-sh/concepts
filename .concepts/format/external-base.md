---
title: External base
description: Another base, used by linking to its files by URL, never by copying it.
---
A [base](base.md) is shared by publishing it; every concept in it then has a URL. A user links to a concept by that URL, pinned to a version, or lists the base under `## External` in the [map](map.md). When an external [concept](concept.md) must be in mind on every turn, the user writes a local concept in their own words with the external concept as its [source](source.md). Because nothing is copied, titles never collide.

Example: a line under `## External` pointing to another repository's `index.md` at a tag.
Source: `SPEC.md`, sections 5 and 6.

## Connections
- Kind of [base](base.md): one that is linked, never copied.
- Requires a [link](link.md) by URL pinned to a version, or a path to a clone outside your own base.
