---
title: Source
description: The file, document or URL that defines a concept authoritatively, named in its body and never copied.
---
A source line, `Source: ...`, gives a reader the place to verify the [concept](concept.md) and keeps the concept correct: when the source changes, the concept is checked against it. A concept points to its source; it never reproduces it. A concept that defines a term with no written source has no source line.

Example: the `Source:` line two lines below.
Source: `SPEC.md`, section 2.

## Connections
- Part of a [concept](concept.md), as one line in the body.
- Used for [external bases](external-base.md): a local concept names the external concept as its source.
